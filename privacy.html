Privacy notice

Who operates the connection

18321752 Canada Inc. operates the Hodi Tea Intelligence Sandbox connection. It is for invited testing with accounts the tester owns or is authorized to connect. This notice takes effect when published.

What the app receives

TikTok supplies the app-specific account identity and display name. If you select public videos from that account, the app requests the selected IDs, creation times, share links, durations and dimensions. The app also records request counts, timestamps, permissions and limited diagnostic information. It uses that information to show the selected metadata and troubleshoot the connection.

Storage and retention

The app stores video observations locally for up to 30 days after a successful observation. A later observation can start a new 30-day window. The content and account credentials are encrypted locally. Identifiers, operational timestamps and receipts may be stored separately. Local backups are managed by the workspace operator.

Pausing and removing access

You can withdraw access in TikTok connected-app settings and request removal of local records or credentials from the workspace operator. Removing access in TikTok does not delete local copies. Do not share your password, authorization codes or tokens when requesting help.

Support site hosting

GitHub Pages hosts these informational pages. GitHub processes ordinary hosting requests under its privacy statement. The pages contain no contact forms, analytics scripts or tracking pixels.
